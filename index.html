Food + Drink

Some classics and favorites from the food side of Aspen. These items should be hyperlinked for ease.

Woody Creek Tavern

This is my favorite! Imagine you go for a bike ride on the Rio Grande trail and land at Woody's a hole-in-the-wall (whatever that means for Aspen) burger joint!

Unravel Coffee

A great casual coffee move with comfy seating and an easy morning energy.

Swedish Hill Bakery

The rooftop cafe at the Aspen Art Museum. One of the prettier low-key views in town.

Sant Ambroeus Coffee Shop

The polished coffee option if you want espresso and pastries with a more refined, Italian feel.

Petit Trois

A chic French lunch and one of the better midday recs if you want something stylish and social.

White House Tavern

Great sandwiches, burgers, salads, and cocktails in a historic cottage. Hard to go wrong here.

Meat & Cheese

Mentioning because people like this spot, but it's a little over-rated. It is a market-style atmosphere with good for an appetizer + cocktails.

Matsuhisa

The classic high-end Aspen dinner move. You'll need a reservation.

Catch Steak

Best if the group wants steakhouse energy with a more modern, louder, scene-y feel.

BOSQ

The Michelin-star tasting-menu option. This is the splurge dinner when the meal itself is the plan.

Casa Tua

A polished Aspen favorite with seasonal ingredients and a private-club atmosphere upstairs.

The Monarch

A classic steakhouse with martinis, moody lighting, and more old-school Aspen energy.

New York Pizza

The easy casual option if you want a slice and a beer instead of turning dinner into a whole event.

Adventure

Both activity and outfitter recs.

Biking

One of the strongest summer recs overall. Look at Silver City Cycle or Ride Aspen if you need gear or help. Road bike to the Maroon Bells, Rio Grande, or Castle Creek,

Hiking

Ajax, Highlands, Buttermilk, and Snowmass all give you a different version of Aspen scenery.

Fishing

Aspen Outfitting Company is our family go-to for guided fly-fishing (we have a friend who is great if you ever need!)

T-Lazy-7 Ranch

A favorite for horseback rides, private fishing ponds, and a more immersive Maroon Bells-area day.

Aspen Blazing Adventures

The broadest operator for rafting, kayaking, jeeping, hiking, Maroon Bells, or a custom adventure day.

Adventure Outfitters

Four Mountain Sports, Aspen Velo, and Ute City Cycles cover bikes. Taylor Creek Fly Shop, Aspen Outfitting Company, and Blazing Adventures cover fishing and rafting.

Nightlife

Aspen's interpretation of nightlife.

Caribou Club

The private-members-club option for dinner, drinks, and dancing if you have access.

GALA

The luxury nightclub move if the goal is a more exclusive, high-energy night.

Silver City

More local and more relaxed, with karaoke, a pool room, and live music.

Aspen Brewing Company

Great for patio beers, a casual start to the night, and a little less formality.

Belly Up Aspen

The best rec for live music. Always worth checking first to see who’s playing.

Hooch + Sterling

Start with cocktails at Hooch, then move to Sterling next door if you want a clubbier finish.

The Living Room

The Hotel Jerome bar is the move if you want refined cocktails and a more classic Aspen room.

Culture

A few cultural stops when you want something more Aspen than just another meal.

Aspen Art Museum

A strong default culture stop, especially if you also want to pair it with Swedish Hill.

Baldwin Gallery

Good for a more design-forward gallery stop in town.

Wheeler Opera House

A local landmark and the obvious rec if you want a performance or a classic Aspen venue.

John Denver Sanctuary

One of the easiest iconic stops if you want something beautiful and low-effort.

Wheeler-Stallard Museum

A good history-focused stop that gives the day a little more depth.

Snowmass Rodeo

This as a unique summer outing with more local-event character.

Signature Events

If a trip lines up with one of these, let it set the whole tone. These are the big summer anchors.

Aspen Ideas Festival

Late June through early July at the Aspen Institute. The best event to build around if you’re into politics, business, media, or culture.

Jazz Aspen Snowmass

A summer-long music anchor with intimate sets and the bigger Labor Day weekend festival.

Aspen Music Festival & School

The classical-music option, running late June through mid-August with a huge range of performances.

Food & Wine Classic

Mid-June and probably the biggest food-focused flex weekend in Aspen. If dining is the priority, this is the one.

The Perfect Aspen Day

If you want one version that feels very Aspen without trying to cram in everything, this is the move.

Morning

Coffee + Aspen Saturday Market

Start at Unravel Coffee or Swedish Hill, then head to the Aspen Farmer’s Market if it’s Saturday.

Midday

One Big Outdoor Plan

Pick one of the real anchors: biking, hiking, paragliding, gondola up Aspen Mountain, or a bigger booked experience through Blazing Adventures or T-Lazy-7.

Afternoon

Lunch + Treat + Happy Hour

Do lunch at the Sundeck, Petit Trois, or White House Tavern, stop at Paradise Bakery, then slide into Clarks or French Alpine Bistro.

Night

Dinner + One Strong Night Move

Book Matsuhisa, Casa Tua, BOSQ, or The Monarch, then choose your finish: Belly Up for music, The Living Room for cocktails, or Hooch into Sterling for more energy.
